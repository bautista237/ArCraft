ⓘ About ArCraft

ArCraft is a NeoForge 1.21.1 mod + web dashboard that records everything that happens on the server and turns it into live stats, leaderboards, achievements, a coin economy, a cosmetics store, clans with chat, and an explorable map — all from a single drag-and-drop jar.

What the mod tracks

While you play, the mod records: PvP kills & deaths (total and PvP-only), damage dealt/taken, mobs killed, blocks mined & placed, items crafted, arrows fired/hit, distance walked/swum/flown/sailed, and the chunks you explore (used to draw the map & heatmaps). Everything shows up on the Dashboard, Rankings, your profile, and the Map.

Recent Activity feed — what gets logged

  • ACHIEVEMENT Stat milestones — crossing 10 / 50 / 100 / 250 / 500 / 1,000 … on PvP kills, mobs killed, blocks mined, blocks placed and items crafted
  • BOSS First kill of the Ender Dragon, Wither, Warden or Elder Guardian
  • ACHIEVEMENT First diamonds mined, first craft of a notable item, first time entering the Nether or the End
  • PVP_KILL Player-vs-player kills (with the weapon used)
  • PLAYER_DEATH Deaths from any cause (with the vanilla death message)
  • CHAT Public in-game chat messages
  • CUSTOM Events an admin logs manually (optionally with a photo)

Each entry shows the player's Minecraft skin face.

How you earn coins 🪙

  • First Ender Dragon kill +100, Warden +75, Wither +50, Elder Guardian +30
  • Each diamond ore you mine +10
  • First craft of a notable item (bow, diamond/netherite gear, beacon, enchanting table…) +25
  • First time entering the Nether or the End +20
  • Stat milestones +5 … +1000 (scaled by the milestone)
  • Admins can grant coins directly

Spend coins in the Store on titles and cosmetic particle trails / glow. Cosmetics can be equipped and unequipped at any time.

Titles

Buy titles in the store, or earn a gold rank title automatically by being #1 in a category: Top Killer, Most Deaths, Explorer (distance), Top Miner, Master Crafter, Top Builder. Titles appear next to your name on the rankings and your profile.

Clans & clan chat

Clans group players and show combined stats. Clan members get a private chat that is synced between the website and the game:

  • /clan <message> or /c <message> — send a message to your clan (spaces allowed)
  • /clan coords — share your current coordinates with the clan

Messages sent in-game appear on the clan page (and vice-versa) within a second or two.

The Map

The Map shows the terrain you've explored, shaded like a Minecraft map, with toggleable heat layers for mining, building and time spent per chunk.

In-game account commands

  • /login <password> — set your web dashboard password
  • /retrieve — privately shows your current password

Cracked (offline) servers show skins by username; premium servers show the official skin. Store cosmetics apply to the in-game player who owns them, so log into the web as your own Minecraft username.
